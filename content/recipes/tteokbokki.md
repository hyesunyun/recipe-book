## Ingredients

### Main Ingredients
- 2 cups rice cakes
- 2 cups water
- 1 stalk of Korean green onions (large scallions)
- 3 sheets of Korean fish cake (어묵)
- 2 hard boiled eggs, shelled (optional)
- oil for frying
- toasted sesame seed (optional)

### Sauce
- 2 Tbsp. gochujang (Korean pepper paste)
- 1.5 Tbsp. gochugaru (Korean chili powder)
- 2 Tbsp. soy sauce
- 3 Tbsp. sugar

## Directions

1. Soak the rice cakes in some water for at least 10 minutes. Slice the green onions and cut up the fish cakes into bite-sized squares/rectangles or triangles.
2. Prepare the sauce by mixing everything in a bowl together.
3. Add some oil to a pot or deep pan and fry up the sliced green onions/scallions for a couple minutes. 
4. Add the rice cakes, water, and sauce.
5. Make sure to stir frequently so the rice cakes don't stick to the bottom. Cook until the sauce becomes thick. 
6. Add the fish cakes. Optional: Add sliced cabbage or other veggies to add more vegetables. Cook until the ingredients cook through. Then, turn the heat off.
7. Add the shelled, hard boiled eggs and coat them in sauce.
8. Optional: add toasted sesame seeds as garnish and serve.
