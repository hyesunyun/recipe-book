## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cup frozen corn kernels, thawed
- 2 eggs
- 2 Tbsp butter, melted
- 1 cup flour
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 2 Tbsp sugar
- 1/2 tsp salt

## Directions

1. Preheat the oven to 350F and grease a loaf pan.
2. Blend 1 cup of corn, the eggs, and melted butter together until a coarse mixture forms. Add the remaining 1/2 cup of corn and pulse the mixture one or two more times. Leave many kernels whole.
3. Whisk together the flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt.
4. Using a large spoon, combine the dry ingredients with corn mixture until a thick mixture forms.
5. Add it to your loaf pan and bake about 30-35 minutes.
6. Let the bread cool slightly before slicing.
